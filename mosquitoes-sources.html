Sources & support resources

Every claim in the video traces to the sources below. Items I could not confirm are listed in the note at the end.

For: Why Do Mosquitoes Prefer Some People?

Sources

How mosquitoes find you and the skin-chemistry study
  1. 1
    Skin compounds associated with attractiveness to mosquitoesNIH Research Matters, 2022, summarizing De Obaldia et al., Cell, 2022. Supports: higher skin fatty (carboxylic) acids in the most attractive people, levels stable for a year or more, some subjects followed for several years, the association-not-proof caveat, and the volunteer with high acids that mosquitoes ignored.
  2. 2
    UW researchers show that the mosquito smells, before it sees, a bloody feastUW News, 2015 (Riffell and colleagues). Supports: only females feed on blood, mosquitoes sense carbon dioxide from up to 30 feet away, and after smelling it they are drawn to a dark dot.
Genes, pregnancy and the myths
  1. 1
    Heritability of attractiveness to mosquitoesFernández-Grandon et al., PLOS ONE, 2015; read through the University of Nottingham repository. Supports: 18 identical and 19 non-identical twin pairs, heritability of about 0.62, the small-sample caveat, pregnant women being more attractive to malaria mosquitoes, and the garlic and vitamin B findings as cited in its introduction.
How big the problem is
  1. 1
    Malaria (WHO Global Health Observatory)WHO. Supports: about 282 million cases and 610,000 deaths in 2024, about 95 percent of deaths in the WHO African Region, and the share of those deaths in children under five.
  2. 2
    Vector-borne diseases (WHO fact sheet)WHO, 26 September 2024. Supports: more than 700,000 deaths a year from vector-borne diseases, and that window and door screens and mosquito nets reduce contact with mosquitoes.
What actually works
  1. 1
    Use insect repellentUS Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. Supports: EPA-registered repellents with DEET, picaridin, IR3535, oil of lemon eucalyptus, PMD and 2-undecanone.
  2. 2
    EPA-registered repellent ingredientsCalifornia Department of Public Health, reproducing the CDC and EPA guidance. Supports: pure essential oil of lemon eucalyptus is not the same as the registered product.
  3. 3
    Electronic mosquito repellents for preventing mosquito bites and malaria infectionEnayati, Hemingway and Garner, Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, 2007. Supports: ten field studies found no effect of electronic repellers on mosquito bites.

A note on wording

This video is health information, not medical advice. For malaria risk, travel plans or symptoms, talk to a doctor or your local health authority. The garden at the start and the end is an invented situation, and the jokes (the menu, the chef-hat bacteria, the tiny binoculars and calculator, the joke tattoo) are illustrations. The video tells these facts as a story without naming the studies in the spoken words, so each claim is traced here. Some lines come from sources I did not open or could not confirm: the Cell paper itself (I read the NIH summary), the idea that skin bacteria feed on skin oil and shape body odor (from press interviews with the researchers), the blood-type line (thin evidence, not settled), the pregnancy explanation of more exhaled air and slightly higher temperature, the idea that loose light clothes are harder to spot and to bite through, and the tips on fans, standing water and heat, sweat and movement. The garlic and vitamin B findings are from the introduction of the twin paper, not from the original trials.
